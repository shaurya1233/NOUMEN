PROFILE — 2026

I am Shaurya Mittal, a student developer and digital creator with an interest in building meaningful experiences through technology.

My journey began with curiosity about how websites work. That curiosity gradually developed into an interest in creating websites, interfaces, digital tools and independent projects.

I explore AI-assisted development as a way to research ideas, accelerate experimentation and bring concepts into reality while continuing to develop my understanding of technology.

My creative interests include web development, interface design, artificial intelligence and digital product experiences.

I value experimentation, independent learning, functional design and the process of improving an idea through repeated refinement.

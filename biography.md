## 03 — DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY

Technology is not simply about writing code. It is about identifying problems, understanding possibilities, designing solutions, and creating something useful.

Shaurya approaches development as an ongoing learning process.

His philosophy is built around the following principles:

### Build with Purpose
Every project should begin with an idea, a clear direction, and a reason to exist.

### Function Before Claims
A digital product should be judged by what it actually does, not by exaggerated descriptions or unsupported promises.

### Design with Intention
Visual presentation, usability, structure, accessibility, and consistency all contribute to the quality of a digital experience.

### Learn Through Experimentation
Practical projects provide opportunities to explore technologies, discover limitations, and improve technical understanding.

### Use AI Thoughtfully
Artificial intelligence can support research, planning, implementation, and problem-solving. It should complement learning and judgement rather than replace them.

### Improve Continuously
Development is iterative. Testing, feedback, refinement, and maintenance are essential parts of creating better products.

## 05 — DEVELOPMENT APPROACH

A typical independent project can involve several stages:

1. **Conceptualisation** — Identifying an idea and defining its purpose.
2. **Research** — Exploring relevant technologies, references, and possible approaches.
3. **Planning** — Establishing the structure, functionality, and intended experience.
4. **Design** — Developing the visual direction and interface.
5. **Implementation** — Translating the concept into working software.
6. **Testing** — Checking functionality, responsiveness, and usability.
7. **Refinement** — Improving the implementation through iteration.
8. **Deployment** — Making the completed project accessible.
9. **Maintenance** — Reviewing and improving the project over time.

This process is flexible. Different projects require different tools, architectures, and development decisions.
